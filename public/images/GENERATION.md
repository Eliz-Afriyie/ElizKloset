# Campaign mockup images

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for this storefront mockup. These are generated illustrations of sample outfits, not confirmed product photography.

Saved assets:

- `public/images/hero-burgundy.png`
- `public/images/hero-blue.png`
- `public/images/hero-champagne.png`

All three images are 1024 × 1536. They are displayed with `object-fit: contain` to preserve the complete model and outfit. Next.js serves optimized responsive versions.

## Final prompt set

The following prompt was used once per outfit, substituting the outfit description below:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ecommerce fashion campaign portrait photograph, tall 2:3 portrait, highest detail. Create a polished editorial photograph of a stylish adult Ghanaian Black woman wearing [OUTFIT], long black wavy hair, subtle gold earrings, holding a small structured handbag. Warm cream boutique interior, soft arch, subtle palm plant, gold accents. Full length composition: entire head and hair with generous headroom, entire outfit and shoes visible, feet fully inside frame, model centered with space on all sides. Natural proportional anatomy. Crisp detailed face, fabric and stitching, professional soft daylight. Palette warm cream, caramel, gold and outfit color. No writing, no text, no logos, no collage, no panels, no interface. Intended as placeholder photography for ElizKloset corporate fashion homepage.

Outfit descriptions:

- Burgundy: a rich burgundy knee-length asymmetric-neck corporate pencil dress with gold button details
- Blue: a royal blue knee-length wrap corporate pencil dress with short sleeves
- Champagne: a champagne cream belted tailored office outfit with short sleeves and pencil skirt
